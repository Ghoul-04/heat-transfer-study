1 · Basic modes

\(q=-kA\dfrac{dT}{dx}\qquad q=-\dfrac{kA}{\Delta x}(T_2-T_1)\)
\(q=hA\,(T_w-T_\infty)\)
\(q=\sigma AT^4,\quad q=\varepsilon\sigma A\,(T_1^4-T_2^4)\)

σ = 5.669×10⁻⁸ W/m²·K⁴ · ε = 1 black body · T بالكلفن للإشعاع

\(\text{°F}=1.8\,\text{°C}+32\quad \text{°R}=\text{°F}+459.69\quad \text{K}=\text{°C}+273.16\quad \text{°R}=1.8\,\text{K}\)

2 · Thermal resistances (Ohm's law)

\(q=\dfrac{\Delta T_{overall}}{\sum R}\)
\(R_{wall}=\dfrac{\Delta x}{kA}\qquad R_{conv}=\dfrac{1}{hA}\)
\(R_{cyl}=\dfrac{\ln(r_o/r_i)}{2\pi kL}\qquad R_{sph}=\dfrac{1/r_1-1/r_2}{4\pi k}\)
\(\text{series: }R=\sum R_i\qquad\text{parallel: }\dfrac{1}{R}=\sum\dfrac{1}{R_i}\)

Interface: \(T_{n+1}=T_n-qR_n\)

2 · Composite / multilayer

\(q=\dfrac{T_{in}-T_{out}}{\frac{\Delta x_a}{k_aA}+\frac{\Delta x_b}{k_bA}+\frac{\Delta x_c}{k_cA}}\)
\(q_r=\dfrac{2\pi L(T_i-T_o)}{\frac{\ln(r_2/r_1)}{k_1}+\frac{\ln(r_3/r_2)}{k_2}+\frac{\ln(r_4/r_3)}{k_3}}\)
\(q_r=\dfrac{4\pi(T_i-T_o)}{\frac{1}{k_1}\left(\frac{1}{r_1}-\frac{1}{r_2}\right)+\frac{1}{k_2}\left(\frac{1}{r_2}-\frac{1}{r_3}\right)}\)
\(k=k_0(1+\beta T):\ q=\dfrac{k_0A}{\Delta x}\left[1+\tfrac{\beta}{2}(T_1+T_2)\right](T_1-T_2)\)

3 · Overall U

\(q=UA\,\Delta T_{overall}\qquad U=\dfrac{1}{\frac{1}{h_1}+\frac{\Delta x}{k}+\frac{1}{h_2}}=\dfrac{1}{R_{total}}\)
\(q=\dfrac{T_A-T_B}{\frac{1}{h_iA_i}+\frac{\ln(r_o/r_i)}{2\pi kL}+\frac{1}{h_oA_o}}\)
\(U_i=\dfrac{1}{\frac{1}{h_i}+\frac{A_i\ln(r_o/r_i)}{2\pi kL}+\frac{A_i}{A_o}\frac{1}{h_o}}\)
\(U_o=\dfrac{1}{\frac{A_o}{A_i}\frac{1}{h_i}+\frac{A_o\ln(r_o/r_i)}{2\pi kL}+\frac{1}{h_o}}\)
\(U_iA_i=U_oA_o=1/\sum R,\quad A_i=2\pi r_iL,\ A_o=2\pi r_oL\)

3 · Critical radius

\(q=\dfrac{2\pi L(T_i-T_\infty)}{\frac{\ln(r_o/r_i)}{k}+\frac{1}{r_oh}}\)
\(\dfrac{dq}{dr_o}=0\ \Rightarrow\ r_c=\dfrac{k}{h}\ \ (\text{max loss})\)

إذا \(r_i<r_c\): العازل الرقيق يزيد الفقد. (كرة: \(2k/h\) — إضافي)

4 · Heat generation (2‑4‑6)

\(\text{wall: }T_0=\dfrac{\dot qL^2}{2k}+T_w,\quad\dfrac{T-T_w}{T_0-T_w}=1-\dfrac{x^2}{L^2}\)
\(\text{cyl: }T_0=\dfrac{\dot qR^2}{4k}+T_w,\quad\dfrac{T-T_w}{T_0-T_w}=1-\dfrac{r^2}{R^2}\)
\(\text{sphere: }T_0=\dfrac{\dot qR^2}{6k}+T_w\)
\(\dot q=\dfrac{q}{V};\ V_{cyl}=\pi r^2L,\ V_{sph}=\tfrac43\pi r^3;\ \ \dot qV=hA(T_w-T_\infty)\)
\(P=I^2R_e,\quad R_e=\dfrac{\rho L}{A_c}\)

5 · Fins

\(\theta=T-T_\infty,\ m=\sqrt{\dfrac{hP}{kA}},\ \dfrac{d^2\theta}{dx^2}-m^2\theta=0\)
\(\text{Case 1: }q=\sqrt{hPkA}\,\theta_0,\quad\theta/\theta_0=e^{-mx}\)
\(\text{Case 2: }q=\sqrt{hPkA}\,\theta_0\tanh mL,\ \ \dfrac{\theta}{\theta_0}=\dfrac{\cosh m(L-x)}{\cosh mL}\)
\(\text{Case 3: }q=\sqrt{hPkA}\,\theta_0\dfrac{\sinh mL+\frac{h}{mk}\cosh mL}{\cosh mL+\frac{h}{mk}\sinh mL}\)
\(\text{thin: }m=\sqrt{\tfrac{2h}{kt}}\quad\text{pin: }m=\sqrt{\tfrac{2h}{kr}},\ A=\tfrac{\pi}{4}d^2,\ P=\pi d\)
\(L_c=L+\tfrac{t}{2}\ (\text{rect}),\quad L_c=L+\tfrac{d}{4}\ (\text{pin})\)

6 · Efficiency & effectiveness

\(\eta=\dfrac{q_{actual}}{q_{max}},\quad\eta_1=\dfrac{1}{mL},\quad\eta_2=\dfrac{\tanh mL}{mL}\)
\(q_{max,rect}=hPL\theta_0,\quad q_{max,circ}=2\pi h(r_{2c}^2-r_1^2)\theta_0\)
\(L_c=L+\tfrac t2,\ r_{2c}=r_1+L_c,\ A_m=tL_c,\ x=L_c^{3/2}\big(\tfrac{h}{kA_m}\big)^{1/2}\)
\(q_{with}=n\,q_{fin}+hA_{between}\theta_0,\quad n=\dfrac{L}{s+t}\)
\(\varepsilon=\dfrac{q_{with}}{q_{without}}=\dfrac{\eta_fA_fh\theta_0}{hA_b\theta_0},\ \ \varepsilon_2=\dfrac{\tanh mL}{(hA/kP)^{0.5}}\)
